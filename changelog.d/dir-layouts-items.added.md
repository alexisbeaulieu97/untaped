A numbered `Dir` (`layout=N`) is a store's layouts again: a registered
install's reference names its layout, `prune()` removes the layouts nothing
holds after the plugin's complete refresh, and doctor's `stores` row lists
them. `Dir(items=..., release=..., moved=...)` lets holders such as projects
hold things in the place, and `items()` is that item store. Holds' own locks
are the `refs` block's named locks, under the home.
