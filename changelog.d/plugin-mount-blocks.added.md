A 12.0 plugin class's caches, directory migrations, doctor checks,
credentials and deprecated behaviours reach `untaped config`, `setup
migrate-dirs`, `doctor` and `auth` under the names its attributes give them
(`motd = Cache(...)` is `hello.caches.motd.max_age_seconds`), and `untaped
plugin schema` prints, and suggests, the record kinds its declaration names.
