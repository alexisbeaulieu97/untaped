# Words

- [plugin]: A piece that is installed and uninstalled on its own, by whoever runs untaped.
- [kernel]: The part of untaped that is there before any [plugin] is installed.
- [contract]: A statement of something a [plugin] can ask for and of what it gets back.
- [choice]: A statement of which [plugin] is used where several could be.
- [instance]: One running copy of a [plugin].
- [host]: The [instance] that started untaped.
- [asker]: Whoever makes an ask.
- [answerer]: Whoever answers an ask.
- [chain]: The asks that led to an ask, in order.
- [content]: What the two sides of an ask send each other, as the ask's [contract] describes it.
- [per-ask]: Said of a [contract] in a [plugin]'s folder when a new [instance] answers each ask for it.
- [staying]: Said of a [contract] in a [plugin]'s folder when one [instance] answers every ask for it; also said of that [instance].
- [adopt], [adopts], [adopted]: untaped adopts a running program it did not start when it counts that program as an [instance] and routes asks to it.
- [description]: The part of a [contract] that says, for people and front ends, what the [contract] and its fields are about, in which order its fields come, and examples of its use.
